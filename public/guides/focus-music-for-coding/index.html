FOCUS GUIDE

Focus music for coding without filling the foreground.

A coding soundtrack works best when it creates continuity while your attention stays on the editor, terminal or problem in front of you.

Start focus music →

Keep the volume predictable

A consistent level reduces the urge to adjust audio repeatedly while you work.

Favor longer sessions

Deep work often benefits from fewer context switches. A stable background track can become part of the environment instead of a new thing to inspect.

Use ambience as an environment cue

Chai ambience and soft environmental sounds can make a coding session feel more like a dedicated workspace.

Take deliberate breaks

Background music cannot replace rest. Pair a focus session with actual breaks so listening stays enjoyable.
