ABOUT

A small digital chai break, built around music.

Chai With Music is an atmospheric web experience for people who like calm background audio: lo-fi beats, study music, focus music, relaxing music and Indian chai-stall ambience.

Explore music & ambience →

What we're building

The idea is simple: make background listening feel like a place, not just a playlist. The interface combines music with visual warmth, chai, steam and ambient sound so the session can fit around studying, working, reading or resting.

Why chai?

Indian tea stalls and tapris are familiar spaces for conversation, quiet work, evening pauses and everyday music. Chai With Music uses that atmosphere as creative inspiration without pretending to reproduce a specific physical place.
